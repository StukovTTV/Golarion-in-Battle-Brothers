# Golarion Localization

*A Pathfinder / Golarion reflavor and contract pack for **Battle Brothers: Legends**.*

Golarion Localization turns each generated Battle Brothers world into a shard of the **Inner Sea** after a cataclysm, with its towns, city-states, noble houses and gods renamed and re-storied into Paizo's **Golarion** setting, and adds a set of hand-authored contracts written to match. The geography is *evocative, not accurate*: every campaign is its own post-cataclysm Inner Sea, not a map of the canon one.

> Status: **in development / playtest.** Playable and stable, still being tuned. Grab the latest build from [Releases](../../releases); per-version notes are in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).

## What to expect

**A reflavored world.** Legends' world generation, renamed and re-storied into Golarion: settlements, the fiend-ruled city-states of the south, noble houses laying claim to the old Runelords of Thassilon, and a pantheon of Golarion's gods. It's a naming-and-story layer; the mechanics underneath are still Legends. Its organizations show up too: the **Pathfinder Society**, the **Sczarni**, and others turn up as patrons and players in the contracts.

**Story-driven contracts.** A growing set of original, multi-stage jobs, each a real little adventure with its own art, branches, and choices that cost something either way (sometimes the right words spare you a fight, and your company's name remembers which way you chose), and each appearing at most once per campaign so a playthrough that finds one won't stumble on it twice. They come in two flavours, and some mix them: straightforward **battle** contracts, where the job is a scripted fight (or a running sequence of them) against whatever the story sets against you; and **choose-your-own-adventure** crawls, where you pick your way through a deck of rooms full of skill checks, traps, and tense decisions, with a fight as one possible turn of events rather than the whole point. The trouble isn't only human, either: alongside bandits, cults, and scheming institutions you'll cross the restless dead, a kobold warren nested in the drains beneath a city, a dragon in adamantine scales holding a village to ransom for its livestock, a drunken ogre who carries off a village's sage for the exile it once drove out, a daemon bound under the floor of a forest shrine by two priests who are no longer there, and stranger things. How often these jobs turn up is up to you: an in-game setting makes them rarer, more common, or off entirely.

**Enemies with their own rules.** The setting's creatures are more than renamed greenskins. Golarion's kobolds field a ten-unit warren drawn from published Pathfinder stat blocks: slingers and snipers, a bomber with alchemist's fire, a trapper who opens with a net, a yapper who screams your line apart, and a dragon-cult of three casters who never travel without a bodyguard. They see further in the dark and worse in daylight, so a warren caught at night is a different fight from the same warren caught at noon. Troglodytes haunt the swamps: ten units from the rank and file up to a Tyrant, with casters of their own and a stench that saps anyone who stands beside them. Boggards squat in the marshes beside them (a slinger, a warrior in studded leather and the swampseer who leads them), and every one of them has a croak that breaks a man's nerve and a tongue that yanks him bodily out of your line.

## Requirements & install

This is a **submod** for Battle Brothers: Legends. You'll need Battle Brothers (with all DLCs), **Legends**, and **MSU** set up and working first. Then drop the latest `mod_golarion_v*.zip` into your `Battle Brothers/data/` folder (don't unzip it) and launch; it loads after Legends automatically. Removing it is just deleting the zip; nothing vanilla or Legends is overwritten.
